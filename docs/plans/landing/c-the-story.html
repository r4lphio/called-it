Why this exists

Write down the call. Grade it later.

Builders make calls all day. This page will convert. That fix cuts errors in half. We ship by Friday. Then the date passes, and nobody checks.

Without a record, "I knew it" and "I was 90 percent sure" mean nothing. Called it gives each call a confidence and a due date, asks you to grade it when the day comes, and shows how often your confidence is right.
